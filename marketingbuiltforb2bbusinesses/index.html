Frequently asked questions

01What makes Carrot different from other agencies?

Carrot combines strategy, branding, design, content, websites, paid media, and growth into one integrated team. Instead of managing multiple vendors, you work with a partner that connects every part of your marketing to help your business become easier to understand, easier to remember, and easier to choose.

02Do you design and build websites?

Yes. Websites are one of our core services. We handle strategy, structure, messaging, design, and development to create a site that looks great and makes your business easier to understand.

03How much does it cost to work with Carrot?

Pricing depends on the scope of the project or ongoing support you need. We offer both fixed project fees and monthly plans, with options designed for growing businesses.

04Do you offer project-based and monthly plans?

Yes. You can hire us for a specific project, such as a website or rebrand, or work with us on an ongoing monthly basis for content, media, strategy, and creative support.

05What types of businesses do you work with?

Our main experience is with B2B companies, particularly in professional services, logistics, manufacturing, technology, and industrial sectors. However, we also work with consumer-facing brands.

06How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on the scope. Most website and branding projects take between four and ten weeks, while smaller creative projects can often move faster.

07Can you help if we already have a brand?

Absolutely. Some clients come to us for a complete rebrand, while others simply need to improve their website, refresh their visual identity, create better content, or strengthen their marketing. We build on what already works and improve what doesn't.

08Do you only work with companies in Texas?

No matter where your business is based, we're here to help. We work with companies across the United States, Mexico, and internationally, making collaboration easy no matter where you are.

Not sure where to start?

Most teams come to us with unclear messaging, underperforming websites, or demand gen that isn’t converting.

We’ll help you identify where the engine is breaking — and what to fix first.
